Motivation & intended impact

The problem

Existing writing datasets focus on surface-level error correction. They cannot model the deeper, iterative process through which writers develop ideas via feedback and real-time dialogue.

0

Public datasets capturing authentic instructional dialogue aligned with multi-draft essays

11

Average instructor comments per essay — richer than any existing public dataset

450hrs

Target audio of student–instructor conferences, a first at this scale

Intended impact

  • Enable AI systems that model higher-order writing development, not just grammar
  • Provide a public benchmark for evaluating and training educational AI feedback models
  • Support equity research on how feedback effectiveness varies across ESL learners, demographics, and institutions
  • Bridge learning sciences and NLP communities through open, multimodal data

What it enables

Research questions

Idea development

How do ideas develop across drafts?

Modeling idea development as a socially mediated, iterative process — distinct from error correction or argument structure.

Feedback strategy

What feedback strategies drive revision quality?

Linking specific feedback types — evaluative, directive, questioning — to measurable revision outcomes.

Equity

Do feedback effects differ across learner backgrounds?

Examining whether feedback types support learning differentially for ESL vs. native speakers and across demographic subgroups.

Dialogue

How does instructional dialogue mediate learning?

Analyzing how real-time spoken interaction predicts subsequent written revision patterns.
